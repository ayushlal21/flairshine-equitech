About Flairshine Equitech

Building financial futures with expertise and trust

Flairshine Equitech began its journey in 2022 as a proprietary trading firm and has since evolved into a trusted name in the investment business. Building on our expertise, we proudly became an official channel partner of IIFL, enabling us to serve a wider range of investors.

Today, we help individuals grow and protect their wealth through a comprehensive suite of financial services, including Stock Trading, Portfolio Management Services (PMS), Mutual Funds, Loans and Insurance Solutions.

At Flairshine Equitech, our goal is simple — to empower every investor with the right opportunities, expert guidance, and personalized financial solutions for long-term growth.
